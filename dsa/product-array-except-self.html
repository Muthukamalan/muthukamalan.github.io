The Core Idea

Every output[i] is just two halves multiplied together: everything to the left of i, and everything to the right of i. Division would compute this in one step (total product ÷ nums[i]) — but that breaks on zeros and is off-limits here.

output[i] = prefix(0..i-1) × suffix(i+1..n-1)

So the trick is to build both halves separately, without ever needing the full product.

Pass 1 — Prefix (left → right): walk forward, and before multiplying nums[i] into the running product, store the running product first. That's everything strictly before i.

Pass 2 — Suffix (right → left): walk backward with a fresh accumulator, multiplying it directly into the output cell already holding the prefix. Same trick, mirrored.

One array, reused as both scratch space and the answer — that's the O(1) extra space.

![breath](/../assets/2026-07-27-breath/default-thumbnail.png)

When you give things space to breathe and be as they are without force, <br>
they fall into the right places and serve you the best.<br>
When we try to use our will to make things be a certain way, <br>
we cause more chaos than peace.<br>
Trust that things will fall into the perfect place and do things honestly.
